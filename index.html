    About, privacy and terms

    Updated October 1, 2026. mbweather.online is a free weather app made by an individual. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no cookies.

    Help

    Can I install it like an app? Yes. In Chrome or Edge, use the Install button in Settings (or the install icon in the address bar). On iPhone, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.

    Does it work offline? The app opens with no signal and shows the last forecast it saved (up to 24 hours old). Live data, radar and the street map need a connection. The simple outline map works offline.

    Do alerts arrive when the app is closed? No. Alerts are only checked while the page is open.

    Privacy: what stays on your device

    The app saves these in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

    • Your home location (name and coordinates) and the place you are viewing.
    • Your settings: units, view, light or dark theme, forecast model, season start date, last tab.
    • Your limits for Windows (on the Plan tab).
    • A list of alert IDs already notified, so you do not get repeats.
    • A saved copy of recent forecasts (up to 3 places, kept 2 hours) so the app opens fast.
    • A flag that you finished setup, and the time of your last backup.
    • The app's own files (the page, its icons and the map libraries) are kept in your browser's cache by a service worker so the app can open without a connection. They hold no personal data. Clear all data removes them too; the app downloads them again on your next visit.
    • If you choose a backup location (Chrome, Edge and similar browsers), a reference to that file, kept in this browser's IndexedDB so Save backup now can overwrite it. The app never reads the file except when you import, and never sees your folder path.

    The developer does not receive any of this. Delete it any time in Data, Clear all data. Export and import are in the same Data menu. A backup file you saved stays where you put it until you delete it yourself.

    Privacy: what is sent to other services

    The app has no server of its own. To work, your browser contacts these services directly. Each one sees your IP address and browser details, like any website, and each has its own privacy policy.

    • Open-Meteo gets the coordinates of the place you view (and a grid of nearby coordinates for map layers), plus the text you type when you search for a place.
    • US National Weather Service (api.weather.gov) gets those coordinates for alerts and the written forecast.
    • NOAA Tides and Currents gets requests for tide data for the nearest station.
    • RainViewer serves radar images. Its requests reveal the map area you are viewing.
    • OpenFreeMap serves street map tiles when you open the Map tab. If it cannot be reached, the app shows a simple outline map instead. Its requests reveal the map area you are viewing.

    Your web host may also keep ordinary server logs (such as IP address) when the page loads. That is outside the app's control.

    Your location. "Use my current location" asks your browser for permission. The app rounds it to about 1 km, saves it on your device and uses it to request weather. Do not enter passwords, Social Security numbers, card numbers or other sensitive information anywhere in this app. It does not need them.

    Notifications. Alerts are checked on your device while the page is open. There is no push service, so nothing arrives when the page is closed.

    Children. The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

    Delete all your data?

    This removes your home location, settings, Windows limits and saved forecasts from this browser. The app will restart setup and forget any backup file locations you chose. This cannot be undone. Save a backup first if you want a copy. Backup files already saved on your device are not touched.
